Introduction

A small studio at the beginning of a bigger journey.

Founded 2026
Based in Czech Republic · Europe
Studio size One independent developer
Engine Unity

Solinthic Games was founded in 2026 as an independent Czech game studio built around a simple idea: start creating, keep learning, and let every new project become a little more ambitious than the last.

Game development had fascinated me for years, but for a long time it felt like something that was simply too large for one person to take on. Programming was only one part of the equation; art, audio, design, testing, publishing and distribution each demanded their own skills and resources.

Today, the tools available to independent creators have changed that equation. What once felt out of reach has become a journey I can actually take — one project, one lesson and one experiment at a time. Solinthic Games is the result of finally deciding to begin.

I make games because I enjoy the process of turning an idea into something people can experience for themselves. I am not trying to pretend that a one-person studio has all the answers. Quite the opposite: the goal is to keep discovering them. With every new game, I want to push a little further — toward projects that are more confident, more distinctive and more original.

Sudoku Shizen is the studio's first release: a deliberately focused first step into game development and publishing, and the starting point of the Solinthic Games journey.
